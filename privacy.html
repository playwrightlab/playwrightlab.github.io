Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

PlayLab is a free educational practice site for browser automation and Playwright testing. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the site.

Information we collect

PlayLab does not require an account to use its practice sections. Some demo forms, local storage examples, and authentication examples process information only in your browser for demonstration purposes.

We may receive limited, aggregated usage information through Google Analytics, such as pages visited, approximate location, device type, and browser type. This information helps us understand whether the site is useful.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use cookies or browser storage for preferences such as theme selection, cookie consent, demo state, and the sample login flow. These features are not used to sell personal information.

Third-party services, including Google Analytics and Google AdSense if enabled, may use their own cookies in accordance with their policies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and the consent controls provided on the site.

Third-party links

PlayLab links to services such as the Playwright documentation, LinkedIn, JSONPlaceholder, and image providers. Those services have their own privacy policies, and PlayLab is not responsible for their practices.

Children

PlayLab is an educational resource and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information on our Contact page.
